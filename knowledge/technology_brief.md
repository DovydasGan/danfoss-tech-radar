# Technology Scouting Radar: knowledge brief

This brief summarises research activity in five technologies relevant to Danfoss: Data Centre Liquid Cooling, Heat Pumps, SiC Power Electronics, CO2 Refrigeration and Electrified Hydraulics. Data comes from OpenAlex, an open index of research papers, retrieved in October 2026. All figures describe published research, not market sales or adoption.

Ranking by growth in share of all research, 2020–2025 (fastest first):
1. Data Centre Liquid Cooling: 38.1% per year relative to all research (45.4% per year in raw paper counts).
2. Heat Pumps: 6.5% per year relative to all research (12.1% per year in raw paper counts).

## Method and definitions

Growth is measured from 2020 to 2025. 'Growth relative to all research' compares each technology's share of all papers published worldwide, which corrects for overall growth in research and for OpenAlex expanding its index in 2025. Organisations, companies and countries cover 2021–2025. Maturity stages follow Technology Readiness Levels: fundamental research (TRL 1–3), lab prototype (TRL 4–5), field demonstration (TRL 6–7) and commercial deployment (TRL 8–9), assigned by Google Gemini from paper abstracts.

## Limitations

Maturity describes where published research is focused, not market maturity: companies rarely publish details of finished products, so research skews towards earlier stages. Publication counts miss patented or confidential work, so company activity is underrepresented. In smaller fields most companies have only a few papers, so rankings beyond the top few are indicative. Technologies are defined by keyword searches, which include some borderline papers.
